# Passwords

Passwords is the page Elegant Passwords opens on. It makes passwords that are hard to guess, easy to remember and fast
to type: each one reads as a short sentence, such as "13 worthy storks openly pet twisty toys", and comes with an
explanation of why it is built the way it is. Everything happens in your browser: nothing you type or generate is sent
anywhere.

The page has three sections side by side: **Options**, **Suggestions** and **Testing**. On a narrow screen or a phone
they become tabs at the top of the page, and you move between them.

## Options

Options is where you say what the site will accept and how you type.

- **Presets** set a site's rules in one tap: a typical site, no spaces, no symbols, a short maximum length, and others.
- **Site rules** are the lengths and the kinds of character the site requires or allows. You can also paste the site's
  own wording ("8-20 characters, at least one number"), and the page shows what it understood and, separately, any
  rule it cannot keep and why.
- **Mode** says how you will type the password, for example with ten fingers or with two, so the suggestions favour
  passwords that sit comfortably under your fingers that way.
- **Requirement options** and **Swaps** fine-tune the sentence: the separator between words, how letters may be
  swapped for digits or symbols, and similar choices.

Changes are a draft until you submit them: a bar at the foot of the page offers "Discard" and "Submit changes".

## Make up a sentence

With **Make up a sentence**, the page invents a sentence for you and builds the passwords from it. The sentence stays
until you press the refresh button beside it.

## Use my sentence

With **Use my sentence**, you type a sentence of your own ("Sally eats sweets") and the page fits it to the site's
rules, shortening long words and adding the digits and symbols the site asks for. Your sentence is kept only in this
browser tab.

## Suggestions

Suggestions lists the passwords made from the sentence, each with its badges: how strong it is for an attacker who
knows nothing, for one who knows English and the usual habits, and a second opinion from the zxcvbn library, along
with how hard it is to type and how easy to remember. "Why?" on a card explains the password. "Refresh" makes new
ones from the same sentence, and "Add" keeps one in My list.

## Testing

Testing is where you try a password before you use it.

- **The typing test** times you typing the password and shows how comfortably it types.
- **My list** keeps the passwords you added, each with its hint, so you can come back to them. On the public website
  it is kept only for your visit.
- **This session** says where the current passwords are kept, and "Forget this session" clears them.
